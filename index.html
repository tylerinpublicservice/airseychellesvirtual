Leadership

A message from our Chairperson

Tyler Nicholas
Chairperson, Air Seychelles Virtual

When I founded Air Seychelles Virtual, I set out to build far more than a Roblox group. I wanted a virtual airline that treats every passenger and every member of staff with the same care, structure, and professionalism you would expect from a real national carrier.

Today that vision is taking shape across PTFS. We operate scheduled flights, trained departments, and full passenger services, from booking and check-in through to boarding and loyalty rewards. Every journey is designed to feel authentic, from the moment you arrive on this website to the moment your flight is complete.

Our goals are clear. We want to be the most realistic and respected virtual airline in the community, to grow a team that takes genuine pride in service, and to give our passengers an experience worth returning for. In the months ahead we will expand our route network, open new career opportunities, and keep improving the systems that power the airline behind the scenes.

To everyone who has joined us so far, thank you. Your support is the reason this airline exists, and it is what will carry us forward. I look forward to welcoming you on board.

Tyler Nicholas
Chairperson, Air Seychelles Virtual
